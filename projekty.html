My projects

Below, you'll find a breakdown of my work into various art and design categories. Click on any image to open it full size.

Logo & Branding

In identity design, my goal is to distill complex brand philosophies into minimalist, powerful visual symbols. Drawing inspiration from classical typography and grid systems, I create logos and branding materials that balance functional modern marketing with timeless aesthetic discipline. Every shape, font choice, and color palette is meticulously calculated to give the brand a distinct, authoritative voice.

Photography

My photographic work serves as an exploration of light, shadow, and human form, deeply rooted in classical academic aesthetics. Whether working in a controlled studio environment or capturing fine art portraiture, I treat the camera as a tool for capturing textures and geometry. This section reflects my experiments with contrast, high-key lighting, and architectural composition, aiming to find silent drama in every frame.

Visual Art

Traditional fine art is the foundation of my entire creative worldview. This curated collection includes academic charcoal drawings, expressive oil paintings, and physical reliefs that explore the tactile relationship between material and metaphor. Working with physical mediums allows me to maintain a direct, tangible connection to artistic heritage, pushing the boundaries of shape and texture before transferring these concepts into the digital space.

Calligraphy & Typography

Calligraphy is a strict discipline of patience, rhythm, and absolute muscle control. My practice spans from classical historical scripts to contemporary abstract lettering. I view text not merely as data, but as a living visual art form where the gesture of the hand dictates the emotion of the word. Through these works, I aim to preserve the craftsmanship of ink and pen, while integrating its principles into modern design layouts.

Digital Art & Concept Design

This digital gallery represents the synthesis of my academic training and digital software proficiency. Utilizing tools like Photoshop, Krita, and Blender, I build fictional worlds, design complex 2D/3D characters, and create stylized book and album covers. From intricate vector portraits to atmospheric concept art, these pieces demonstrate how classical understanding of anatomy and depth transforms digital canvases into compelling realities.
