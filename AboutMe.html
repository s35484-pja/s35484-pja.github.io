Who am I?

I am a graphics student at the Polish-Japanese Academy of Computer Technology. I work on creative projects involving logo design, commissioned drawings and paintings, any projects related to calligraphy and typography, digital art, illustration, cover design, 2D and 3D character design, as well as video editing. For me, every project is a way to express myself and an opportunity to overcome any challenges brilliantly. I put my heart and soul into my work and love what I do, because my work is also my favourite pastime. As a child, I attended art school, then pursued self-development in this field, and now I am studying my favourite subject professionally, so art has been with me my whole life and I have no intention of limiting myself to just one area; instead, I try my hand at all kinds of creative projects.
