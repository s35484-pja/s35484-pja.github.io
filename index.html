Behind the Art

Art has been the defining thread of my entire life. What started as a childhood fascination in art school has matured into a professional pursuit of visual storytelling. To me, graphic design, illustration, and digital art are more than just skills—they are my absolute favorite pastime, my way of thinking, and how I express my world.
I believe that a true creator shouldn't be boxed into one discipline. That is why my work spans across completely different worlds: from the raw, physical textures of traditional oil painting and relief work to the precise, vector lines of branding and dynamic video editing. I find immense joy in the strict rules of classical calligraphy, just as I do in the endless possibilities of 3D character design in Blender or Maya.
I put my heart and soul into every piece, treating every client commission or academic project not just as a task, but as a challenge to be solved brilliantly. Welcome to my space—where tradition meets digital innovation.

What I do:

  • Fine Art & Lettering: Academic drawing, oil painting, conceptual reliefs, and custom typography/calligraphy.
  • Digital Media: 2D/3D character design, digital illustration, and cover artwork.
  • Design & Motion: Logo design, identity branding, and creative video editing.
